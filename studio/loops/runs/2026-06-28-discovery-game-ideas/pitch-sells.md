# A. Driftbell Valley

## What is it?

**You plant pinwheels in a pastel meadow. Wind spins them. You collect the sparkles they drop. Bigger pinwheels + windier valleys = faster collecting forever.**

Think Bee Swarm's field grind — but the "bees" are pinwheels you designed, and the satisfaction is *watching them spin*.

## First 5 minutes

You spawn on a soft green hill under a cotton-candy sky. A friendly **Windmill Keeper** hands you one starter pinwheel — pink-and-white stripes, three blades.

1. **Plant** it on your plot (one click).  
2. A gentle breeze spins it. **Drift tokens** — little glowing spirals — pop out and bounce on the grass.  
3. You run over them. *Ding.* Currency goes up.  
4. The Keeper says: *"Plant three more and I'll show you the craft bench."*  
5. You plant, spin, collect. By minute 4 you've crafted a **blue pinwheel** that spins 20% faster.  
6. Minute 5: you unlock the **Meadow Row** — a second strip of land. You already want a *fourth* pinwheel color.

**Core verb is obvious:** spin → collect. No tutorial wall.

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | Fill your starter plot (6 pinwheel slots). Learn that **running past a pinwheel** gives it a manual spin burst (skill moment — like Rally Kart micro-joy between grinds). |
| 15–30 min | Unlock **Craft Bench Tier 2** — pinwheels with 4 blades, higher token rate. First **Keeper Quest**: *"Plant 2 pink + 2 yellow in a square."* Reward: a **Breeze Sprite** pet that auto-spins one pinwheel while you're elsewhere. |
| 30–45 min | Open **Gust Hill** — wind tier 2 zone. Pinwheels here are bigger but need stronger wind (upgrade: **Wind Vane** on your plot). Tokens per spin jump 3×. |
| 45–60 min | First **Gust Day** event warning: *"Wind shifts east in 10 minutes!"* You rearrange pinwheel angles (simple rotate) for bonus drops. You leave the hour with **12 pinwheels**, **2 Breeze Sprites**, and a half-finished mandala pattern on your plot. |

**Bee Swarm parallel:** Gust Hill = blue flower field. Breeze Sprite = first bee. Keeper Quests = bear quests. Gust Day = Beesmas-style rotating event.

## Why you come back tomorrow

- Pinwheels **keep spinning offline** (capped daily drip — no infinite idle exploit, but "welcome back" loot).  
- **Weekly pinwheel blueprint** in the shop (earnable; rotates — always a new chase).  
- **Mandala completion** — arranging colors in patterns unlocks plot cosmetics (visible flex, no PvP).  
- **Wind tier 3** gated behind quest chain — you see the valley in the distance from day 1.

You never "beat" the sim. You unlock windier, prettier, spinier.

## Co-op moment (optional live 2P)

Your friend joins. They don't need their own progress to help.

- **Co-plant:** Friend can place pinwheels on the *shared edge* of your plots (3 slots). Their pinwheels spin for *your* tokens when you're both online — bonus 15% drop rate.  
- **Spin chain:** Run past each other's pinwheels in sequence for a **Combo Spin** — tokens multiply for 10 seconds. Feels like a co-op rally lap, not a raid.  
- Friend leaves? Your plot still works 100% solo. Their edge pinwheels vanish cleanly — no griefing.

## Solo-not-lonely

- **Windmill Keepers** walk your plot and comment (*"That mandala is coming along!"*).  
- **Visitor NPCs** (traveling pinwheel collectors) stop by with mini-requests.  
- **Async gifts:** Send a pinwheel *skin* postcard to a friend — they get a free cosmetic, no trade scam risk.  
- Server-wide **Wind Festival meter** — everyone contributes spin tokens; when full, global 2× wind for 15 min. You feel part of something without chatting.

## Unlock dopamine (kids + EP)

Kids love **spinning things** and **collecting sparkly drops** — this is tactile and visual, not spreadsheet sim.

Chase ladder:

1. New pinwheel **colors/patterns** (craft)  
2. Bigger **blade tiers** (upgrade)  
3. New **wind valleys** (zone gate)  
4. Rare **Gust Day** tokens (event)  
5. **Mandala** completion trophies (completionist)  

Unlocking new stuff every session — your grilling feedback.

## Money (fair F2P)

| Earn in-game | Robux optional |
|--------------|----------------|
| All pinwheel tiers, valleys, Breeze Sprites | **Drift coins** packs (currency — skip grind) |
| Event tokens | Private server for co-op with family |
| Cosmetics from mandalas | Time-skip on *craft bench* cooldowns only |

**No** paid random eggs. **No** multipliers you can't eventually earn. Robux = convenience, not power permanently locked.

## v1 vertical slice (Phase 06 target)

**One meadow biome + Gust Hill + craft bench 3 tiers + 1 Breeze Sprite type + Gust Day event skeleton.**

EP builds: 6 pinwheel models (3 blade counts × 2 palettes), meadow terrain, Keeper NPC, token VFX.  
Agents build: plant/spin/collect, offline drip, quest chain, co-plant edge slots.

Playtest bar: *"I planted one more pinwheel before logging off."*

## Why it's not generic

Roblox has snowball rollers and garden planters. **Nobody owns pinwheel-field collector.** The fantasy is instantly readable in a thumbnail: candy pinwheels on a hill, sparkles flying. Spinning is inherently satisfying (physics toy + sim numbers).

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm's addiction, but your collectors are pinwheels you arranged — and the whole valley looks like a Ghibli hillside.*

# B. Snowglobe Shelf

## What is it?

**You shake snowglobes on a cozy bedroom shelf. Each shake releases tiny scene pieces. Collect them, complete the diorama inside the globe, earn sparkle tokens, buy more globes.**

It's Pet Sim's "gotta catch 'em all" — but each pet is a **miniature world you built** and display.

## First 5 minutes

You wake in a pastel bedroom. Warm lamp. Rain on the window (cosmetic, cozy). A wooden **shelf** with one empty snowglobe slot glowing softly.

1. The **Curator Mouse** gives you **Globe #1: Forest Clearing** (empty — foggy inside).  
2. Click globe → **shake** (hold mouse / tap). Mini leaves, a mushroom, a tiny bench fly out as **fragments** — glowing puzzle pieces orbiting the globe.  
3. Catch fragments (walk into them). Each *slots* into a silhouette inside the globe.  
4. *Pop* — the forest scene snaps together. Globe now shows a complete tiny diorama with gentle snow inside.  
5. **Sparkle tokens** reward. Buy **Globe #2: Beach Hut** from the shelf shop.  
6. Minute 5: you're shaking Globe #2 while Globe #1 **passively drips tokens** on the shelf. Two-globe collector fantasy starts.

**Core verb:** shake → catch → complete.

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | Complete Forest + Beach globes (2/12 in starter set). Learn **fragment rarity** — golden bench piece is 1/20 shakes. |
| 15–30 min | Unlock **Shelf Room B** (cozy attic). 4 new globe slots. First **Mouse Quest**: *"Complete a globe with a water element."* Reward: **Shake Charm** (+10% rare fragment luck, earnable). |
| 30–45 min | **Peek mode** unlocked — zoom camera *into* completed Forest globe. 30-second walkable diorama. Kids lose their minds. You take a screenshot. |
| 45–60 min | **Blizzard Night** event starts — only tonight, *aurora fragments* can drop. You don't feel FOMO stress: event runs 48 hours, gentle UI. You finish hour with **5 globes**, attic half-full, peeked into 3 worlds. |

**Bee Swarm parallel:** globes = bee types. Fragment rarity = pet rarity. Shelf rooms = map zones. Blizzard Night = seasonal event.

## Why you come back tomorrow

- Incomplete globes **remember partial progress** — always something to finish.  
- **New globe blueprint** each week (earnable from Mouse Quest chain).  
- Completed globes **drip sparkle tokens forever** — your shelf is a passive engine (like Bee Swarm's honey production).  
- **Shelf visitor schedule** — NPCs arrive, favorite a globe, leave a gift. Async delight.

## Co-op moment (optional live 2P)

- **Co-shake:** Friend joins. Both hold shake on a **Mega Globe** (2-player only, 2× fragments, split loot fairly).  
- **Shelf tour:** Walk friend's bedroom instance (read-only) — admire globes, leave a sticker reaction. No stealing.  
- Solo? Mega Globes unavailable — you lose a bonus, not core progress.

## Solo-not-lonely

- Curator Mouse **narrates** your shelf growth.  
- **Visitor NPCs** (owl librarian, kid neighbor) request globe themes.  
- **Global showcase board** — async, post your favorite globe screenshot slot; likes earn cosmetic frames.  
- Blizzard Night is **server-wide** — chat optional, vibe shared.

## Unlock dopamine

**"Unlocking new stuff"** — your exact kids feedback — is the entire game:

1. New **globe themes** (forest → beach → space tea shop)  
2. Rare **fragment variants** (golden mushroom!)  
3. **Shelf rooms** (bedroom → attic → observatory)  
4. **Peek dioramas** (show-off)  
5. Event-only **aurora fragments**

Every unlock is a tiny **world** not a stat line.

## Money (fair F2P)

| Earn in-game | Robux optional |
|--------------|----------------|
| All globes, rooms, charms | Sparkle token packs |
| Event fragments (during window) | Private server |
| Cosmetic shelf frames | Shake cooldown skip (optional) |

Fragment drops are **free random** — no odds disclosure needed for Robux because you don't buy random pulls with Robux.

## v1 vertical slice

**Bedroom shelf + attic room. 12 globe templates. Peek mode for 3 globes. 1 Blizzard Night event stub.**

EP builds: globe props, 12 diorama fragment sets (low-poly mini assets — your Blender sweet spot), bedroom set.  
Agents build: shake/catch/slot, passive drip, quest chain, co-shake.

Playtest bar: *"I need the golden bench fragment."*

## Why it's not generic

Snowball sims roll. Gardens grow. **Nobody collects diorama fragments inside shakeable snowglobes on a bedroom shelf.** The thumbnail sells itself: hand holding globe, tiny world inside, sparkle burst.

**Sell line:** *Pet Sim collection addiction + Wizard Tycoon "my space" pride — but every collectible is a miniature world on your shelf.*

# C. Chime Orchard

## What is it?

**Walk through a pastel orchard of wind chimes. When they ring, you catch tone shards. Complete melodies in a codex. Your orchard literally plays the music you earned.**

Bee Swarm meets a relaxing sound garden — **your progress changes what you hear**.

## First 5 minutes

You enter **Row One** — bamboo chimes hanging from peach trees. Soft pentatonic notes when wind blows.

1. A chime rings. A **tone shard** (glowing music note) floats down. You catch it.  
2. Codex opens: slot shard into **Melody #1** (3 notes). *C – E – G*. Third note completes it — gentle harp flourish, Row One ambient track updates.  
3. **Listener Bear** appears: *"Find a D shard for Melody #2."*  
4. You walk Row Two (unlock at 5 shards). Different chime material = different timbre.  
5. Minute 5: first **Breeze Sprite** quest unlocked — *"Collect 10 shards in Row One."* Reward: sprite that auto-rings a chime every 30 sec.

**Core verb:** hear ring → catch tone.

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | Complete Melodies #1–3. Orchard soundtrack layers in (your codex = your mixtape). |
| 15–30 min | Unlock **Glass Row** — crystal chimes, rare harmonic shards. First duplicate shards → sell for **Breeze coins**. |
| 30–45 min | Assign Breeze Sprite to Glass Row. AFK ring while you quest in Bamboo Row. Classic Bee Swarm passive collector moment. |
| 45–60 min | **Wind Festival** preview — server gust boosts **gold harmonic shards** for 20 min. You chase one missing note. Hour ends with **6 melodies**, **2 sprites**, orchard sounds distinctly *yours*. |

## Why you come back tomorrow

- **Melody codex** is endless — seasonal sets (Spring Pentatonic, Winter Bells).  
- **New rows** = new biomes (Bamboo → Glass → Copper → Starlight).  
- **Your orchard music** evolves — emotional attachment competitors can't copy with reskins.  
- Listener Bear quest chains escalate like Science Bear — hard grinds, big rewards.

## Co-op moment (optional live 2P)

- **Duet row:** Row Seven requires two players to trigger chimes in sequence (alternating notes). Bonus harmonic shards. Solo player uses **Echo Stones** (earnable item) to play both sides — slower but complete.  
- **Never blocked solo.**

## Solo-not-lonely

- Listener Bears **react** when you complete melodies (applause emote).  
- **Async melody postcards** — send a 3-note jingle to a friend; plays when they log in.  
- Wind Festival meter — server collaboration without chat.

## Unlock dopamine

- New **chime materials** (sound + visual change)  
- **Melody completions** (audible reward — rare in Roblox sims)  
- **Breeze Sprites** (collectors)  
- **Row biomes** (zone unlock)  
- Event **gold harmonics**

## Money (fair F2P)

Breeze coins earnable; Robux buys coin packs + private servers. All melodies/chimes earnable. No paid random tone gacha.

## v1 vertical slice

**3 orchard rows + 9 melodies + 2 Breeze Sprite types + Wind Festival stub.**

EP builds: chime models (bamboo, glass, copper), tone shard VFX, orchard terrain.  
Agents build: ring detection, codex UI, audio layering system (cap 4 simultaneous voices).

Playtest bar: *"I need one more note to finish the melody."*

**Risk:** Audio must stay cozy — volume cap, pentatonic-only v1.

## Why it's not generic

Wind chimes on Roblox today = **decor in someone else's garden**. This makes sound **the loot**. Your orchard is a generative soundtrack — progress you can *hear*.

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm grind depth, but every upgrade changes the music in your personal sound garden.*
