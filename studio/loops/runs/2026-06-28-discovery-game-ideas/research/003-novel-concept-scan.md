# 003 — Novel Concept Scan (Iteration 1)

**Run:** 2026-06-28-discovery-game-ideas  
**Agent:** Market Research Analyst  
**Trigger:** EP Q33 redirect — reject repo-sourced pitches; require all-new, never-made-on-Roblox concepts

## EP redirect (verbatim)

> Find 3 all-new (e.g. not referenced anywhere in this repo) and novel (e.g. never made before) roblox game.

## Method

1. Invent candidate verbs/fantasies **not present** in `studio/docs/discovery/` or prior run research mapping to named pitches
2. WebSearch each candidate against Roblox game titles and core mechanics
3. Reject any concept with a direct Roblox sim/collector equivalent
4. Retain concepts that pass uniqueness + EP brief constraints (cozy pastel, solo+co-op, one core verb, fair F2P, sim/collector anchor)

## Cozy sim trend alignment (iteration 1)

Cozy simulators outperform pure clickers in 2026 by offering interconnected progression, discovery, and optional social play — not frantic tapping ([TheInfoBytes](https://theinfobytes.com/why-cozy-simulators-are-outperforming-clicker-games-on-roblox)).

All three passed concepts use **one core verb** with Bee Swarm-style depth layers (collectors, fields/zones, quests, rotating events) without copying bee/garden/pet templates.

## Iteration 1 recommendation

Proceed to synthesize **Chime Orchard**, **Driftbell Valley**, **Snowglobe Shelf** as Final 3 iteration 1.
