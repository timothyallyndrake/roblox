## Opportunity framing

EP brief constraints narrow the search space:

- **Must:** All-ages cozy pastel, solo-complete + optional live co-op, one core verb, fair F2P, novel on Roblox
- **Anchor:** Simulator/collector with Bee Swarm DNA (fields, collectors, quests, events)
- **Avoid:** Hacker sim, PvP siege, edgy themes, pay-to-win

This section maps **where white space exists** within those bounds.

## Gap 1 — "Bee Swarm fantasy reskin" with distinct visual identity

**What's crowded:** Generic gardens (Grow a Garden), pets/eggs (Pet Sim).

**What's open:** Bee Swarm's **field + collector + quest + event** loop applied to a **non-bee, non-generic-garden** fantasy with EP's cozy pastel art direction.

**Candidate:** Starlit Conservatory — cosmic plants, constellation codex, moth/firefly collectors instead of bees. Commercial precedent (Grow a Garden) validates plant collection; constellation twist avoids looking like a GAG clone.

**Risk:** Must launch with unmistakable visual identity (glass domes, night sky, glowing flora) — thumbnail war is brutal in sim genre.

## Gap 2 — Exploration treasure hunt + player storefront

**What's crowded:** Pure grind sims without spatial exploration.

**What's open:** **Adventure between grinds** — EP explicitly wants this (Bee Swarm blue quest fields energy). Treasure hunt + arranging finds in a cozy shop is **not directly occupied** by a top-50 title.

**Candidate:** Hearth & Haul — explore pastel biomes → stock personal storefront → NPC/customer sales → fund upgrades.

**Market signal:** Kids loved "unlocking new stuff" (grilling Q20). Exploration + discovery satisfies that without PvP siege.

**Risk:** Two-system scope (expedition + shop). v1 must ship **one biome + one shop room** before expanding.

## Gap 3 — Gentle timed-service cozy loop

**What's crowded:** Stressful tycoon timers, combat-survival hybrids.

**What's open:** **Prep → short expedition → serve → upgrade** rhythm without combat. Think Animal Crossing shop hours meets Bee Swarm gather runs.

**Candidate:** Glider's Rest — waystation hub, glider island runs, traveler arrivals.

**Risk:** Timed "train arrival" must feel cozy not FOMO-stressful; needs playtest tuning.

## Monetization landscape (sim genre)

| Pattern | Prevalence | EP alignment |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| Robux currency packs | Universal | ✅ Allowed (time-skip) |
| Gamepass VIP multipliers | Common | ⚠️ Must be earnable equivalent |
| Paid random eggs/pets | Common | ⚠️ Requires odds disclosure; prefer earnable hatch |
| Private servers for co-op | Growing | ✅ Fits optional co-op |
| Battle pass / season pass | Emerging | ✅ If rewards earnable via play |

Roblox paid-random rules require odds disclosure when Robux buys random outcomes. **Design around earnable random rewards** (free drops) + Robux for non-random convenience.

Source: `studio/docs/compliance/roblox-policy-summary.md`, [Roblox paid random items docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/monetization/paid-random-items)
