## Executive summary

Simulation is the largest Roblox genre by concurrent users (~4.3M CCU) and the most represented in the top 50 (16 titles). Incremental simulators and tycoons dominate the accessible, all-ages lane. Platform discovery now rewards **28-day retention** over short spikes — a structural tailwind for EP's "endless progression, never beat the sim" constraint. Grow a Garden proved plant/collection sims can reach historic scale but also saturated the cozy-farming lane; Bee Swarm remains the EP reference for field-grind + quest + event DNA at a smaller but durable CCU (~18–24K).

## Platform incentives (discovery & monetization)

### Discovery algorithm (June 2026)

Roblox expanded Recommended For You signals from **7-day to 28-day** windows:

| Signal | Importance | Implication for our pitch |
|--------|------------|---------------------------|
| D1 retention | High | Strong onboarding / first 60s (minimize bounce rate) |
| D2–7 retention | High | Enough content depth in week 1 |
| D8–28 retention | High | Rotating events, quest chains, unlock tiers |
| Play-through rate (PTR) | High | Icon + thumbnail + promise match |
| Intentional co-play | Medium | Optional live co-op is a platform-positive signal |
| Robux spend days | Medium | Fair F2P still monetizes via convenience |

Sources: [DevForum — RFY algorithm update](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/recommended-for-you-algorithm-improvements-that-better-value-long-term-retention/4684575), [Roblox newsroom — Optimizing Discovery](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox)

### Creator economics

- Creators earned **$1.5B+ via DevEx in 2025**
- **42% DevEx rate increase** for eligible spend by age-verified US 18+ players (June 8, 2026) — targets "novel games" with deeper gameplay
- **Standout Games** section on Home for novel, high-retention titles

Source: [Roblox newsroom — DevEx 42% increase](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)

**Strategic read:** Our EP brief targets all-ages cozy sim — the mass-market lane Simulation already owns. We compete on **retention depth + visual identity + fair monetization**, not on chasing 18+ DevEx bonuses (though co-play parents may age-check).

## Bee Swarm Simulator — reference DNA

EP's #1 reference game. Design patterns that drive retention:

| Pattern | Implementation | Our takeaway |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| Core verb | Collect pollen → convert to honey → upgrade bees | One clear verb with depth |
| Fields/biomes | Color-coded fields with escalating requirements | "Plots" or "zones" as progression gates |
| Collectors | Bees multiply output passively | Companion/collectible multipliers |
| Quest chains | NPC bears with escalating grind quests | NPC quest givers + translators/unlocks |
| Rotating events | Beesmas seasonal event (weeks-long) | Seasonal content prevents "beat the sim" |
| Fair premium | Event catalog uses earned + premium currencies | Everything earnable; Robux = skip/grind only |

Sources: [Grokipedia — Bee Swarm](https://grokipedia.com/page/Bee_Swarm_Simulator), [Beesmas 2025 guide](https://www.beeswarmsimulator.net/events/beesmas)

## Implications for RGS discovery loop

1. **Market is open** for simulator/collector — largest genre, proven monetization.
2. **Differentiation required** — plant sim lane crowded post–Grow a Garden; need distinct fantasy (constellation greenhouse, treasure shop, waystation).
3. **Retention > virality** — design for D28, not launch spike; rotating events + endless unlock matrix.
4. **Fair F2P is competitive advantage** — EP frustration with P2W aligns with growing parental scrutiny (Kids/Select accounts).
5. **Optional co-op is platform-positive** — intentional co-play days boost discovery; solo must remain complete.
