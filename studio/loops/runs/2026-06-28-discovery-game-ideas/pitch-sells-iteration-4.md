# A. Sortstop Depot (8.65)

**What is it:** A bright, cheerful **parcel depot** on your own plot. Packages roll in on a gentle conveyor; you **sort** each one into the color-coded chute that matches its pictogram slip. Fill **order cards** to earn depot stamps → upgrade belts, unlock new depot floors.

**Core verb:** **SORT** (route packages to correct chutes — not collect floating loot)

**First 5 min:** Spawn at counter → tutorial package arrives → drag to green chute → *ding!* → first order card fills 1/3 → unlock second chute lane → see **Gift Wrap Floor** door glow.

**First hour:** Run Morning Rush + Gift Wrap shifts; hire **Sorter Arm** (Bee Swarm collector analogue) on one lane; open second floor with fragile stickers (bonus stamps); **Double Stamp Hour** event.

**Co-op:** **Split Belt** — you handle left two chutes, friend handles right two; shared order card progress. Solo: **Lazy Susan** upgrade (earnable) auto-rotates slow packages for easier grabs.

**Why tomorrow:** New order card sets weekly; depot layout = flex; offline = queued packages cap at mailbox (open game to sort burst).

**v1:** Ground floor + Gift Wrap floor, 12 package types, 1 Sorter Arm, Double Stamp Hour stub.

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm field energy, but the "pollen" is packages and the skill is sorting — not chasing sparkles.*

# B. Ridgeline Patches (8.58)

**What is it:** You're a **trail keeper** on forest ridges. Hike short loops to find **viewpoint markers**; gaps in boardwalks and rope bridges need **patch tiles** you place to reconnect the route. When hikers complete your patched trail, you earn **trail badges** and unlock the next ridge.

**Core verb:** **PATCH** (lay trail tiles to connect viewpoints — not collect motes)

**First 5 min:** Spawn at trailhead → find broken boardwalk (3 missing planks) → place planks from pack → first hiker crosses → badge earned → **Fern Overlook** marker appears on map.

**First hour:** Patch 2 ridge loops; **Trail Scout** NPC assigns "link 3 viewpoints" quest; **Fall Foliage** event (hikers tip 2× badges); unlock **Mist Bridge** zone with rope tiles.

**Co-op:** **Co-patch** — both players must place one tile on the same gap within 10s for sturdy bridge (bonus badges). Solo: **Extra Plank** tool (earnable) extends reach.

**Why tomorrow:** New ridge zones = new viewpoints; trail layout is player expression; hikers keep walking finished routes while you're offline (capped badge drip).

**v1:** Trailhead + Fern Overlook + Mist Bridge, 9 viewpoint sets, Fall Foliage event stub.

**Sell line:** *Wizard Tycoon "build your plot," but you're building hiking trails — and hikers pay you in badges when the path works.*

# C. Kiln Shelf (8.52)

**What is it:** A sunny **pottery counter** with a wheel out back. **Throw** simple pots (timing tap), **glaze** patterns from your palette, line them on your **display shelf**; NPC shoppers buy favorites and leave tips. Upgrade wheel, glazes, and shelf rooms.

**Core verb:** **GLAZE** (craft + stock shelf — not field collect)

**First 5 min:** Tutorial throw one pot → pick blue glaze → swipe stripe pattern → place on shelf → first customer buys → 5 coins → unlock second glaze color → **Window Shelf** slot opens.

**First hour:** 8 pot shapes thrown; **Kiln Helper** (auto-fires one shape while you're away); complete **Market Day** order card (3 polka-dot mugs); open **Garden Room** with outdoor display shelf.

**Co-op:** **Co-throw** — one player spins wheel rhythm, other taps shape timing for bonus quality tier. Solo: **Steady Foot** pedal (earnable) widens timing window.

**Why tomorrow:** Weekly customer "favorite shape" rotation; shelf layout flex; offline kiln fires queued pots (cap).

**v1:** Front counter + Garden Room, 10 shapes, 6 glaze patterns, Market Day stub.

**Sell line:** *Cook & Sell's shop warmth meets Plane Crazy's "I made this" — but the craft is pots on a shelf, not chasing drops.*
