# A. Candlewick Mantle (8.73)

**What is it:** A cozy cottage hearth with pastel candles. As they burn, **warm motes** rise from the wax glow. Catch them, fill your **Flame Album**, upgrade candles, unlock new mantle rooms.

**First 5 min:** Spawn at hearth → one candle lit → motes float up → catch 10 → unlock second candle → Flame Album page 1 complete → see **Side Mantle** door glow.

**First hour:** 6 candles lit; **Wick Sprite** companion (Bee Swarm collector) auto-gathers from one candle; open **Reading Nook** mantle room; **Candlelight Hour** event boosts golden motes.

**Co-op:** **Co-warm** — friend stands at opposite mantle; both candles sync for 2× motes 60s. Solo: **Snuffer Charm** (earnable) gives same bonus slower.

**Why tomorrow:** Candles burn offline (capped motes); weekly candle blueprints; album endless; mantle layout = flex.

**v1:** Hearth room + Reading Nook, 15 candle sets, 1 Wick Sprite, Candlelight Hour stub.

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm grind, but the loot is warm light from candles you arranged on your mantle.*

# B. Bellgrass Lane (8.63)

**What is it:** Pastel meadow paths where **bell-grass** jingles when you walk. Each jingle drops **chime motes**. Collect, upgrade boots, plant more bell-grass rows on your lane plot.

**First 5 min:** Walk path → *jingle jingle* → motes pop → catch → buy **Soft Boots** → second grass row unlocks on your plot.

**First hour:** 4 grass rows; **Bell Sprite** auto-jingles one row; unlock **Hilltop Lane** (windier = more motes); **Wind Chime Afternoon** server event.

**Co-op:** **Duet Walk** — sync steps with friend on shared row for combo jingles. Solo complete via **Metronome Stone** (earnable).

**Why tomorrow:** Bell-grass grows offline; new grass **timbres** (album); kids love sound + walking.

**v1:** Meadow Lane + Hilltop, 12 timbres, 1 Bell Sprite, event stub.

**Sell line:** *Your field is a musical path — every step is collect, upgrade, jingle faster.*

**Note:** Distinct from **Chime Orchard** (fixed orchard chimes) — here *you* trigger grass bells by walking.

# C. Sunshard Chapel (8.58)

**What is it:** Tiny pastel chapel. Rotate **stained glass** panels as sun moves; colored **light puddles** appear on the floor. Walk through puddles to collect **sunshards**; complete glass sets in a **Prism Codex**.

**First 5 min:** Enter chapel → tutorial rotate one panel → blue puddle spawns → collect 5 shards → first codex pane fills → **Rose Window** slot unlocks.

**First hour:** 3 windows aligned; **Prism Keeper** NPC quests; **Morning Ray** event (gold shards); open **Side Aisle** room with second window wall.

**Co-op:** **Co-turn** — two players rotate opposite panels for rare **rainbow puddle** (15s). Solo: **Sun Dial** item slows sun for easier align.

**Why tomorrow:** Daily sun path = new puddle patterns; codex endless; EP Blender showcase for glass panels.

**v1:** Nave + Side Aisle, 9 window sets, Morning Ray event.

**Sell line:** *Collect sunlight like Bee Swarm pollen — but the fields are colored light on a chapel floor.*
