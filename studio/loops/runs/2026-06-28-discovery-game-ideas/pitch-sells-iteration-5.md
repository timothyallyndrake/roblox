# A. Curio Counter (8.68)

**What is it:** A cozy **alley trinket counter** with a tiny dig patch out back. Unearth curios, **scrub** them at your sink, slap on a price tag, and stock the front **window shelf**. NPC browsers buy favorites; fill your **Curio Codex** sets to unlock new dig layers.

**Core verb:** **SCRUB** (clean finds for sale — not collect floating motes, not mass sand-dig grind)

**First 5 min:** Spawn at counter → tutorial dig patch yields one muddy brooch → scrub minigame → tag 10 coins → place in window → first NPC buys → **Back Alley** dig spot unlocks.

**First hour:** 3 codex sets started; hire **Shop Goblin** (Bee Swarm collector) to auto-scrub one item slot; **Weekend Rush** event (2× browser traffic); open **Side Window** display room.

**Co-op:** **Co-scrub** — friend holds item steady while you scrub for faster shine tier. Solo: **Soft Brush** (earnable) widens scrub sweet spot.

**Why tomorrow:** New codex themes weekly; window layout = flex; offline = muddy items queue at sink (cap).

**v1:** Front counter + Back Alley dig, 15 curio types, 1 Shop Goblin, Weekend Rush stub.

**Sell line:** *Treasure hunt meets storefront — you dig it up, scrub it shiny, and your window sells it while you dig the next one.*

**EP fit:** Directly answers Q28 treasure + shop idea with a new name and shop-first loop (not Hearth & Haul reskin).

# B. Skyline Post (8.60)

**What is it:** Pastel **sky islands** connected by launch ramps. Strap a mail satchel, **glide** through floating **mailbox hoops**, drop packages on landing pads. Earn post stamps → upgrade wings, unlock higher islands.

**Core verb:** **GLIDE** (skill delivery runs — not sort belts, not field collect)

**First 5 min:** Launch from Ramp 1 → steer through 3 hoops → land on Pad A → 5 stamps → buy **Wide Wings** → see **Cloud Loop** ramp glow.

**First hour:** Clear 4 island routes; **Draft Sprite** follows you and carries one bonus satchel; **Tailwind Hour** event; unlock **Sunset Archipelago** (windier, more stamps).

**Co-op:** **Tandem Glide** — friend launches you with extra boost; shared route timer for bonus stamps. Solo: **Feather Brake** (earnable) slows glide for easier hoop threading.

**Why tomorrow:** Weekly route rotations; stamp album endless; short runs = Rally Kart "one more try" between upgrades.

**v1:** Starter archipelago + Sunset zone, 8 routes, 1 Draft Sprite, Tailwind Hour stub.

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm quest energy in 90-second glider runs — deliver mail through sky hoops, upgrade wings, fly farther.*

# C. Wobble Cart (8.55)

**What is it:** Village **market haul** sim. At the farm gate, **stack** produce crates on your wheelbarrow, then **balance-walk** the path to the town stall without tipping. Unload for market coins → bigger cart, longer routes, heavier loads.

**Core verb:** **STACK** (physics balance haul — not patch trails, not glaze pots)

**First 5 min:** Pick 3 apples → stack on barrow → walk slow path → unload at stall → 8 coins → buy **Wide Tray** → **Berry Lane** route unlocks.

**First hour:** 5 routes cleared; **Stable Wheel** upgrade; **Market Day** event (2× coin tips); unlock **Hilltop Stall** with bumpier path.

**Co-op:** **Co-stack** — friend places crates while you steady the barrow before walk. Solo: **Balance Pole** (earnable) reduces wobble.

**Why tomorrow:** New produce types each week; route mastery leaderboard (cosmetic); offline = barrow pre-loaded with easy route (cap coins).

**v1:** Farm gate + Town square + Hilltop, 10 produce types, Market Day stub.

**Sell line:** *Plane Crazy physics joy on a cozy walk to market — stack it high, wobble carefully, get paid.*
