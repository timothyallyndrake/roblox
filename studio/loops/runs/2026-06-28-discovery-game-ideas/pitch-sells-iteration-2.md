# A. Mothlight Hollow

## What is it?

**At twilight, gentle moths gather around your meadow lanterns. Catch their soft glow-motes, fill the lamps, and expand a cozy moth sanctuary — each new lamp unlocks a darker, prettier grove.**

Pet Sim's chase + Flutter Starlight's moth love — but on Roblox as a **field collector sim** with Bee Swarm structure.

## First 5 minutes

1. Spawn in **Lantern Meadow** at dusk (pastel purple sky, firefly-like but slower, cozier).
2. NPC **Hollow Keeper** gives you a **Paper Lantern** and a **Moth Jar** (cosmetic, not trapping — motes are friendly light).
3. A **Lunar Moth** lands on your lantern. It sheds 3 **glow-motes**. You walk through them — *soft chime*, currency up.
4. Keeper: *"Fill this lantern to 10 motes and I'll give you a second lamp post."*
5. Minute 5: two lamp posts lit on your plot, first **Moth Codex** entry unlocked (Lunar Moth silhouette + fact card). You see **Twilight Row** gated in the distance.

**Core verb:** catch glow-motes (walk-through collection, no net violence).

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | Light 4 lamp posts; unlock **Moth Codex** tier 1 (5 species silhouettes). |
| 15–30 min | First **Keeper Quest**: attract 3 **Copper Moths** (plant moonflower bait — one click, passive). Reward: **Lantern Sprite** auto-collects motes on one post while you're elsewhere. |
| 30–45 min | Open **Twilight Row** — deeper blue biome, rarer moths. Duplicate motes → **Hollow coins**. |
| 45–60 min | **Moonrise Event** starts (48h window): **Silver Moth** motes drop. You end with 8 lamp posts, 12 codex entries, 1 Lantern Sprite. |

## Why tomorrow

- Moths **visit overnight** (offline motes cap — welcome-back glow).
- Weekly **Moth Calendar** (real moon phase cosmetic — earnable, not paid).
- Endless codex (100+ moth patterns across biomes).
- Lamp layout = expression (Wizard Tycoon plot pride).

## Co-op (optional 2P)

**Co-light:** Friend helps "call" moths at your plot edge — both players stand at two lanterns, 2× mote drop for 60 sec. Solo? Use **Echo Bait** (earnable) for same bonus alone (slower).

## Solo-not-lonely

- Hollow Keeper commentary; **moth-watcher NPCs** visit lit plots.
- Async **mote postcards** (send a glow trail to friend's plot).
- Server **Moonrise meter** — community event, no chat needed.

## Unlock dopamine

New moth species → new lamp glow color → new biome. Kids see **living light** around their plot.

## Fair F2P

All moths/lamps earnable. Robux = Hollow coins + private servers.

## v1 slice

Lantern Meadow + Twilight Row, 20 moth types, 1 Lantern Sprite, Moonrise event stub.

## Why not generic

No Roblox moth **collector sim** found. Distinct from Chime Orchard (audio) — this is **visual twilight + gentle nature**.

**Sell line:** *Bee Swarm fields, but your collectors are moths and your progress is a glowing sanctuary at dusk.*

**Composite: 8.60**

# B. Puddleglint Village

## What is it?

**After gentle rain in a pastel village, puddles hold prismatic glints. Splash them, catch the glints, upgrade boots and gutters, unlock rainier districts.**

The tactile joy of jumping in puddles — turned into a collector sim with zero combat.

## First 5 minutes

1. Spawn on **Cobble Lane** — soft rain, pastel shop fronts, umbrellas optional cosmetic.
2. **Puddle Warden** kid NPC: *"Try the big puddle!"*
3. Jump/splash — rainbow **glint orbs** burst upward. Catch 5. *Sparkle sound.*
4. Spend glints on **Rubber Boots Tier 2** (bigger splash radius).
5. Minute 5: second puddle lane unlocked on your **Village Row** plot; first **Glint Album** sticker placed.

**Core verb:** splash → catch glints.

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | Upgrade splash radius; fill Glint Album page 1 (6 puddle shapes). |
| 15–30 min | **Rain Bell** event — server drizzle increases glint rarity 10 min. Quest: splash 50 glints. |
| 30–45 min | Unlock **Uphill Quarter** — steeper lanes, bigger puddles, better drops. |
| 45–60 min | First **Puddle Pal** (floating droplet companion) auto-splashes small puddles on your row. |

## Why tomorrow

- **Rain schedule** rotates district bonuses (not FOMO — 24h cycles announced).
- Glint Album endless (shape + color variants).
- Boot/gutter upgrades = visible bigger splashes (satisfying feedback).

## Co-op (optional 2P)

**Mega Splash:** Both players jump same puddle on 3-2-1 — **Glint Burst**. Solo equivalent: **Boot Stomp** ability (cooldown).

## Solo-not-lonely

- Village NPCs cheer; **Glint Parade** NPC event when server album milestones hit.
- Async: leave **puddle chalk** art on friend's lane (cosmetic).

## Fair F2P

All districts/boots earnable. Robux = glint coin packs.

## v1 slice

Cobble Lane + Uphill Quarter, Glint Album 24 entries, 1 Puddle Pal, Rain Bell event.

## Why not generic

Rainy Vibes = social hangout. This is **splash-collect-upgrade** sim loop. Not stone-skip distance competition.

**Sell line:** *Jump in puddles forever — each splash feeds an album of rainbow glints and upgrades your cozy village row.*

**Composite: 8.50**

# C. Steamcup Loft

## What is it?

**A pastel attic windowsill lined with teacups. When cups steam, catch the rising **steam wisps** before they fade. Complete cup sets, earn cozy tokens, expand the loft shelf.**

Wizard Tycoon "your space" + collector sim — steam is the loot.

## First 5 minutes

1. Spawn in **Attic Loft** — rain on window, warm lamplight, 3 teacups steaming.
2. Click cup → steam rises as **wisp orbs** (slow float). Walk into wisps — collect.
3. **Tea Mouse** NPC: *"Fill the Blue Willow cup set (5 wisps) for a new cup slot."*
4. Complete set — new cup appears, starts steaming passively.
5. Minute 5: 5 cups on sill, first **Steam Album** page done, **Loft Row 2** shelf slot visible.

**Core verb:** catch steam wisps.

## First hour

| Time | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| 0–15 min | 8 cups steaming; unlock **Honey Steam** cup (golden wisps). |
| 15–30 min | **Tea Time Quest** chain from Mouse; reward: **Kettle Spirit** heats cups faster on one shelf. |
| 30–45 min | Open **Skylight Bay** window section — outdoor cups, wind affects wisp paths (gentle skill). |
| 45–60 min | **Afternoon Steam** event — lavender wisps. 12 cups, 2 shelf rows, 1 Kettle Spirit. |

## Why tomorrow

- Cups **steam offline** (capped wisp drip).
- Weekly **Tea Set Blueprint** (earnable).
- Shelf arrangement = cozy flex; peek into completed cup sets for micro-diorama label art.

## Co-op (optional 2P)

**Co-steam:** Both players catch wisps from a **Grand Teapot** — shared wisp fountain, split collect fairly.

## Solo-not-lonely

- Tea Mouse visits; **Village Tea Club** NPCs rate your shelf.
- Async steam postcards (scent note = flavor text gift).

## Fair F2P

All cups earnable. Robux = steam tokens.

## v1 slice

Attic Loft + Skylight Bay, 15 cup sets, 1 Kettle Spirit, Afternoon Steam event.

## Why not generic

Cafe management sims exist off Roblox; **steam wisp catching on a windowsill collector** not found on Roblox.

**Sell line:** *Your attic is a Bee Swarm field made of teacups — catch the steam, complete the sets, grow the coziest shelf on Roblox.*

**Composite: 8.55**
