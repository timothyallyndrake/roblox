# Cosmic Bloom — v1 Content Catalog

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Status:** Stable pre-build names and roles; art, icons, and exact reward amounts remain tuneable.
**Scope:** Full v1 prototype content slots.

---

## Catalog Rules

- Names here are implementation stubs, not final asset art.
- No entry adds paid random, trading, stealing, or progression skips.
- Cosmic Spin Legendary items are cosmetic or standard-strength decor skins. They must not outperform earnable buff decor.
- Prize Blooms are display trophies only.
- Bloomdex star-map slots remain 21; the wider Bloomdex index tracks all breeds and mutation discoveries.

## Base Breed Mutation Catalog

Each base breed has three named mutation entries at launch. Signature map mutations are marked `map`.

| Base breed | Mutation 1 | Mutation 2 | Mutation 3 |
|------------|------------|------------|------------|
| Glow Mote | Shimmer Glow Berry `map` | Dewdrop Glow Berry | Lantern Glow Berry |
| Moon Melon | Giant Moon Melon `map` | Crescent Moon Melon | Lullaby Moon Melon |
| Nebula Sprout | Void Nebula Pod `map` | Speckled Nebula Pod | Bubble Nebula Pod |
| Sun Peach | Golden Sun Peach `map` | Freckled Sun Peach | Honeywarm Sun Peach |
| Star Carrot | Striped Star Carrot `map` | Curly Star Carrot | Torch Star Carrot |
| Blaze Blossom | Double Blaze Blossom `map` | Ember Blaze Blossom | Softflame Blaze Blossom |
| Flare Mint | Spicy Flare Nectar `map` | Frosted Flare Nectar | Spark Flare Nectar |
| Dusk Lace | Woven Dusk Lace `map` | Velvet Dusk Lace | Misty Dusk Lace |
| Twilight Pod | Glowing Twilight Pod `map` | Sleepy Twilight Pod | Ripple Twilight Pod |
| Eclipse Fern | Silver Eclipse Fern `map` | Shadowcap Eclipse Fern | Halo-Tip Eclipse Fern |

Mutation rules:

- First discovery unlocks a Bloomdex index entry.
- Only `map` mutations light star-map slots.
- Mutated harvests grant +1 Stardust over normal harvest.
- Mutations are never sold directly for Robux.

## Prize Bloom Catalog

Prize Blooms are rare trophy results from splice luck and deep chains. They are not a fruit source, not sold at the Outpost, and not duplicated into delivery inventory.

| ID | Prize Bloom | Source | Display role |
|----|-------------|--------|--------------|
| P01 | Skyknot Prize Bloom | C12-leaning rare outcome | First deep-chain trophy |
| P02 | Moonlace Prize Bloom | Lumina pair mutation roll | Little Slipper display option |
| P03 | Solar Mane Prize Bloom | Sun Rawr chain rare outcome | Warm lion-themed case |
| P04 | Silverloom Prize Bloom | Silver Weaver chain rare outcome | Woven twilight case |
| P05 | Comet Kiss Prize Bloom | Meteor pollen rare outcome | Event trophy |
| P06 | Keeper's Kindling | Rank 5 capstone eligible roll | Story trophy option |

Rules:

- Launch cap remains 3 display cases, 1 Prize Bloom each.
- Extra case slots are not sold in v1.
- Swapping a case does not destroy the Prize Bloom.
- Prize Blooms can appear in visit stats and screenshots, not leaderboards.
