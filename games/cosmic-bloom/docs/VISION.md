# Vision — Cosmic Bloom

> **Display name locked:** Cosmic Bloom (2026-06-28)  
> **Slug:** `cosmic-bloom`

## One-line pitch

Breed cosmic plants in **open starlit meadows**, fill the **Bloomdex**, deliver **multi-line supply orders** at the **Orbital Outpost**, join **Community Feed** events, chase **night meteor showers**, and upgrade your **garden**, **Stellar Nursery**, and **buff decor**.

## Core loop

```
Plant → nurture (sun / moon) → harvest distinct bloom fruits & pollen →
cross-breed / mutations in Stellar Nursery → Bloomdex stars →
personal supply orders + Community Feed at Orbital Outpost →
earn Cosmic Coins → upgrade → repeat
```

## EP constraints

- Solo-first; friends on same server, own gardens
- Cozy Fair F2P — **no paid random**; **Spin Tokens earned only**; Robux = **passes + time-skip + cosmetics** (§6)
- **No P2P trading** in v1

## Bloomdex *(launch)*

Invented constellation-*like* names; **literal collection shapes** with **21 star-map slots** (10 bases + 10 signature mutations + 1 prize). **12 cross-breds** in tiered splice matrix (§1.2b). **Full Bloomdex index** tracks ≥3 mutations per base. **Post-MVP:** completed collections in night sky above plot.

## Planets *(v1: 3 × 3 fruits)*

**Lumina** → **Solara** (Lumina R10) → **Glimmer** (Solara R15) → **Launch Pad** (Glimmer R20, post-MVP). Delivery gated linearly; **grow any fruit you legitimately unlock anytime**. Multi-line supply orders; infinite rounds.
