# Cosmic Bloom — Phase 04 Creative Bible

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Purpose:** Art/UI direction before Roblox production begins.
**Audience:** Art Director, UX/UI Designer, Asset Pipeline Specialist, Producer, EP.

---

## Creative North Star

Cosmic Bloom should look like a **cozy cosmic meadow**, not a lab, casino, sci-fi factory, or generic farm.

Players should understand the game from one image:

> A kid-friendly Roblox meadow under a soft starry sky, full of glowing magical plants, planet-feeding gadgets, and warm places to gather without danger or theft.

---

## Style Pillars

| Pillar | Direction |
|--------|-----------|
| Open starlit meadow | Big sky, soft grass, visible gardens, no enclosed domes. |
| Cozy cosmic pastoral | Space magic mixed with garden warmth: lanterns, pollen, paths, soft glow. |
| Invented sky shapes | Constellation-like UI uses fantasy shapes only; no real sky maps. |
| Friendly wonder | Round forms, readable silhouettes, gentle sparkle, low threat. |
| Social comfort | Hub feels like a safe town square, not a competitive marketplace. |

---

## Palette

| Use | Colors | Notes |
|-----|--------|-------|
| Night meadow base | Deep blue, violet, soft teal | Avoid black-heavy spooky night. |
| Moonbound plants | Blue, lavender, pearl, mint | Gentle glow, soft bloom VFX. |
| Sunbound plants | Gold, peach, coral, warm green | Bright but not harsh neon. |
| Twilight plants | Silver, indigo, rose, dusky cyan | Elegant and slightly mysterious. |
| Hub accents | Warm cream, brass, starlight yellow | Keeps space tech from feeling cold. |
| Warning/urgent UI | Soft amber, never red panic | Events should invite, not stress. |

---

## Lighting Rules

- Use soft rim light on plants so gardens read at night.
- Stars should feel magical and readable, not realistic astronomy.
- Meteor events can be brighter, but avoid flashing patterns that overwhelm.
- Cosmic Spin spectacle should feel like a celebratory toy, not a slot machine.
- Community Table should be warm, food-like, and communal.

---

## Plant Silhouette Rules

| Plant family | Silhouette language |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Moonbound | Rounded, sleepy, glowing, low-to-mid height. |
| Sunbound | Upright, warm, bouncy, leaf points like rays or manes. |
| Twilight | Draped, woven, arcing, slightly taller, silver edges. |
| Cross-breds | Clearly inherit parent shapes; one obvious new trait. |
| Mutations | Same base silhouette plus one visual twist; do not become unreadable. |
| Prize Blooms | Larger, more composed, trophy-like, but not delivery fruit. |

Readability target: a kid should identify a plant family from 20 studs away.

---

## UI Tone

- Friendly labels, large touch targets, low clutter.
- Bloomdex star maps should feel like a sticker book / constellation poster.
- Outpost order UI should read like a shopping list, not an invoice.
- Community Table UI should say "help together," not "hurry or fail."
- Paid items need plain language: what it does, what it does not do.
- Use "earned" language for Spin Tokens.

---

## Hub Vs Garden Energy

| Space | Feeling | Visual notes |
|-------|---------|--------------|
| Player garden | Personal, calm, expressive | Lower density, room for decor, clear plots. |
| Stellar Nursery | Magical workshop greenhouse | Organic glassy glow is OK, but no enclosed garden dome fantasy. |
| Central hub | Safe town square | Clear paths to Outpost, Seed Stand, Cosmic Spin, Meteor Merchant. |
| Orbital Outpost | Warm delivery kiosk | Capsule launch VFX, planet icons, friendly signage. |
| Community Table | Cozy potluck | Big readable table, fruit pile progress, soft crowd space. |
| Meteor crash sites | Temporary wonder | Sparkly crater, personal crates, no loot scramble. |

### Orbital Outpost

Warm brass-and-starlight delivery booth. Needs:

- Capsule loading bay.
- Friendly planet icon display.
- Personal order panel.
- Community Table adjacency or clear path.
- Launch VFX that says "you helped" immediately.

### Community Table

Large shared table with three fruit-line slots and visible fill meters. It must feel like a potluck. Avoid countdown panic, harsh failure states, or competitive winner podiums.

### Cosmic Spin

Physical hub board, not a full-screen casino wheel. Needs:

- Clear "1 free daily" / "Spin Token" entry.
- Current Legendary display.
- Recent wins strip.
- Earn-only token icon.
- Celebration VFX that reads magical, not gambling.

### Meteor Merchant

Temporary night vendor with a friendly cart or blanket stall. Should feel like "special visitor after meteors," not black-market trader.

### Seed Stand

Simple, trustworthy seed booth for common starters. Avoid premium-store framing.

### Stellar Nursery

Personal greenhouse/workbench hybrid. Needs:

- Pollen input slots.
- Gentle reveal chamber.
- Upgrade cues for slots, speed, and luck.
- Organic cozy feel; avoid hard sci-fi lab.

4. Community Table fruit potluck.
5. Stellar Nursery plant discovery.

Do not lead with Cosmic Spin as the primary thumbnail.

---

## Anti-Rules

- No glass domes.
- No real-world constellation names or copied real sky maps.
- No automated moth/firefly collectors.
- No hard sci-fi lab tone.
- No casino, slot machine, or jackpot-first framing.
- No theft, trading, combat, or griefing imagery.
- No scary space void, horror night, or sharp alien silhouettes.
- No UI that implies paid spins or paid random seed packs.
- No premium store treatment for Seed Stand.
- No tiny unreadable mobile-first text.

- Starter plant silhouettes approved.
- Three Bloomdex collection visual directions approved.
