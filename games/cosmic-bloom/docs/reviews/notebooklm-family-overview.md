# Cosmic Bloom — Family Audio Overview Source

This document is meant to be uploaded into NotebookLM or read aloud to kids and family reviewers. It explains the game idea in plain language and asks for honest reactions.

---

## What Is Cosmic Bloom?

Cosmic Bloom is a cozy Roblox game about growing magical space plants in your own open meadow under the stars.

You plant seeds, help them grow with moonlight and sunshine, harvest glowing fruit, mix pollen in a Stellar Nursery to discover new plants, and fill a Bloomdex with fun star-shaped collections. You also feed friendly planets at the Orbital Outpost, join server events at the Community Table, chase meteor showers at night, and decorate your meadow with cozy cosmic items.

The big promise is simple:

**Your garden is yours. Nobody steals your plants. Nobody tricks you with trades. You can play solo, with friends, or in a private meadow with family.**

---

## The Fantasy

Imagine you wake up in a quiet meadow with stars overhead. A friendly Constellation Keeper tells you that faraway planets are hungry, and your garden can help.

Your first little plant is called Glow Mote. It grows Glow Berries. When you harvest enough, you carry the berries to the Orbital Outpost and launch a capsule to feed the planet Lumina. The game celebrates that you helped planet friends.

As you play, you discover more plants. Some love moonlight. Some love sunshine. Some like both. You collect pollen and use the Stellar Nursery to mix plants together. Sometimes you discover a brand-new plant. Sometimes you find a special mutation, like a Shimmer Glow Berry or a Giant Moon Melon.

Every discovery helps fill your Bloomdex. The Bloomdex is like a plant collection book, but the collections are shaped like pictures made of stars. The first ones are Little Slipper, Sun Rawr, and Silver Weaver.

## What You Do In The First 10 Minutes

1. You arrive in your meadow and meet the Constellation Keeper.
2. The Keeper gives you a Glow Mote seed.
3. You plant it in your garden.
4. You use moonlight to help it grow.
5. You harvest Glow Berries and pollen.
6. You take the Glow Berries to the Orbital Outpost.
7. You launch your first food capsule to Lumina.
8. Lumina's planet friends get fed, and you earn Cosmic Coins.
9. The Keeper gives you Moon Melon and Nebula Sprout seeds.
10. You use the Stellar Nursery to mix pollen and discover Starlace Vine.
11. Your Bloomdex lights up its first star.
12. The Keeper says, "The meadow is yours. Feed the stars."

By the end of the tutorial, you should understand:

- How to plant.
- How to harvest.
- How to feed planets.
- How to mix plants.
- Why the Bloomdex matters.
- What you might want to do next.

## Why It Should Be Fun

Cosmic Bloom is meant to feel relaxing, exciting, and safe.

It is relaxing because your plants grow over time, your meadow is pretty, and there is no fighting.

It is exciting because you can discover new plants, find mutations, complete star maps, see meteor showers, and win cool cosmetic prizes from Cosmic Spin.

It is safe because other players cannot steal your crops or take your progress. Pollen swabs from friends are gentle samples. They do not remove fruit, pollen, or progress from the garden owner.

It is social because players can gather at the Community Table, run to meteor showers together, and watch big Cosmic Spin wins at the hub. But you can still play by yourself and make progress.

## The Main Places

### Your Meadow

This is your personal garden. You plant seeds, harvest fruit, place decorations, and show off special Prize Blooms in display cases.

### Stellar Nursery

This is where you mix pollen from two plants to discover new plants or special mutations.

### Bloomdex

This is your collection book. It tracks your plants, mutations, and star-shaped collections.

### Orbital Outpost

This is where you deliver fruit to feed friendly planets. Personal orders help you earn Cosmic Coins.

### Community Table

This is a server event where players bring fruit together. It should feel like a cozy potluck, not a stressful emergency.

### Meteor Merchant

At night, meteors can fall. Everyone gets their own meteor crate, and the Meteor Merchant can appear with special items.

### Cosmic Spin

Cosmic Spin is a shared prize board in the hub. You get one free spin each day, and extra spins use Spin Tokens that you earn by playing. You cannot buy spins with Robux.

### Private Meadow

A Private Meadow is an invite-only server for friends and family. It does not make plants grow faster or give better rewards. It just makes the shared Community Table goal smaller so a small family group can still help together.

---

## What Players Can Buy

Cosmic Bloom should not feel like a game where paying is required.

Players can earn progress by playing. Robux purchases are meant to save time or add cosmetics, not skip the heart of the game.

Important promises:

- No paid random seeds.
- No buying Cosmic Spin pulls.
- No paid stealing.
- No trading.
- No buying exclusive plant breeds.
- No private server advantage except a smaller shared Community Table goal for small groups.

One item to listen for is Extra Garden Beds. It gives two extra plant beds. That might sound helpful, but it should not sound unfair. If it sounds too powerful, say so.

## Short Glossary

**Bloomdex**
Your plant collection book. It fills in as you discover plants and mutations.

**Stellar Nursery**
The place where you mix pollen to discover new plants.

**Orbital Outpost**
The hub booth where you feed friendly planets with fruit from your garden.

**Community Table**
A shared server event where everyone can add fruit to help complete a goal.

**Prize Bloom**
A rare trophy flower you display in your meadow. It is not sold or used for planet orders.

**Stardust**
A helpful upgrade item you collect from harvesting plants.

**Cosmic Spin**
A shared prize board in the hub. Extra spins are earned, not bought.

**Private Meadow**
An invite-only server for friends and family. It keeps the same growth, rewards, and gates as public servers.

**Cosmic Coins**
The main game money you earn by feeding planets and playing.

**Comet Shards**
Event currency from meteor showers and Community Table rewards.

**Spin Tokens**
Earned items that give extra Cosmic Spin turns.

## Final Listener Note

The goal is not to say every idea is perfect. The goal is to catch confusing names, boring parts, unfair-sounding purchases, stressful events, and the coolest moments before the game gets built.
